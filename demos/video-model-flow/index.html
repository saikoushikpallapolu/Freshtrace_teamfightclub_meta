What is live, recorded, or illustrative?

  • All four required models were actually called for this recorded run.
  • SAM processed the video; Spark analyzed its visual sequence. Wonder read the saved video observations through a real tool loop.
  • The source is silent. Voice input is separately synthesized demo speech, then transcribed by the real Muse Voice model.
  • Timeline exploration, task creation and confirmations here run in your browser. They do not make new model calls.
  • No sensor evidence or group-localization trial was fabricated. Muse Image is outside this evidence workflow and was not used.
